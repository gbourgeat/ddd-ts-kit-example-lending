### alert-rule (domain service), in unit

`unit/alert-rule.service.spec.ts`

- **AlertRuleService**
  - holds no state of its own
  - raises an alert when the early repayments within a year of the payout pass 10,000
  - raises no alert when the early repayments within a year of the payout stay at 10,000
  - does not count an early repayment made after a year from the payout
  - raises no alert on a repayment that is not early, however large
  - raises an alert on a payment from an account that is not the borrower's
  - raises no alert on a payment from one of the borrower's accounts
  - gives the early repayment reason when both rules are broken
