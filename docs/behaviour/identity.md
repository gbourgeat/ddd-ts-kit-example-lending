### member (aggregate), in unit

`unit/member.aggregate.spec.ts`

- **Member**
  - joins an organisation in a role, and announces it
  - is rehydrated in its stored state without recording a new fact
- **The role of a member**
  - changes, and the change is announced with the role it leaves
  - stays as it is, silently, when it is asked again
  - becomes, or stops being, owner only by the decision of an owner
  - stays owner when the organisation has no other owner
  - is never changed by the member themselves, nor above the role of who decides
- **A member**
  - is removed, and the removal is announced
  - who owns the organisation is removed by an owner only, and never when they are its last owner
  - leaves by themselves, and is removed by no one below their role; the delegation used is named
  - who was removed is not found any more
