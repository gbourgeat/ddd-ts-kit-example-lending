### scoring-model (aggregate), in unit

`unit/scoring-model.aggregate.spec.ts`

- **ScoringModel**
  - is born with its identity and no version
  - is rehydrated in its stored state without recording a new fact
  - numbers the first published version 1, with its label and model risk
  - numbers each next version after the last one published
  - records the fact of each publication, with the version it announces
  - refuses a blank label and publishes no version
  - records nothing as long as no business intention is expressed
