### instalment-schedule (domain service), in unit/ledger

`unit/ledger/instalment-schedule.service.spec.ts`

- **InstalmentScheduleService**
  - holds no state of its own
  - gives 299.71 a month for 10,000.00 at 5.0 percent over 36 months
  - gives 223.64 a month for 5,000.00 at 6.9 percent over 24 months
  - gives 367.43 a month for 20,000.00 at 3.9 percent over 60 months, without the monthly fee
  - divides the amount evenly over the months when the rate is zero
  - gives as many instalments as months, the first ones constant
  - lets the last instalment absorb the rounding difference: 299.69 for the example of 10,000.00
  - sums the instalments to the amount plus the interest, the balance cleared at the last month
  - refuses an amount that is not above zero
  - refuses a negative rate
  - refuses a number of months below one
