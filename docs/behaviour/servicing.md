### loan.aggregate.behaviour, in unit/ledger

`unit/ledger/loan.aggregate.behaviour.spec.ts`

- **A loan paid out to its borrower**
  - starts its ledger on the instant of its payout
  - records its payout as the fact that opens its ledger, with its identifier and instant
- **A loan closed once its ledger is over**
  - records its closure on the instant it is closed
  - refuses to close a loan that is already closed, and records nothing
- **A loan whose instalments fall due**
  - records each instalment of its schedule that has fallen due, with its number and its due date
  - records the instalments due on the instant of the run, and no instalment not yet due
  - records nothing twice when the same run is repeated
  - records only the instalments that fell due since the last run
  - records no instalment once the loan is closed
- **A loan repaid early**
  - records the capital repaid, leaving the rest outstanding
  - is closed by the same ledger when the whole outstanding capital is repaid
  - refuses a repayment above the outstanding capital, and records nothing
  - refuses a repayment of nothing, and records nothing
- **A loan written off**
  - records the outstanding capital written off, and the loan ends
  - refuses a second write-off, since the loan is closed
  - is reopened by a reversal, its outstanding capital open again
  - refuses a reversal of a loan not written off
