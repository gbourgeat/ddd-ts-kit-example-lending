### register-business (handler), in unit

`unit/register-business.handler.spec.ts`

- **Register business**
  - records the business of a known customer, and saves it with the facts of its registration
  - announces the registration of the business once it is saved
  - answers the identifier of the customer whose business is registered
  - refuses a registration for a customer that is not known, with the code UNKNOWN_CUSTOMER
  - refuses a second registration of the business, with the code BUSINESS_ALREADY_REGISTERED
  - keeps the first business and announces nothing more when a second registration is refused
  - is refused to an actor who does not hold the permission business.register
