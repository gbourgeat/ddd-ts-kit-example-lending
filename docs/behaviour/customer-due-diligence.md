### customer (aggregate), in unit

`unit/customer.aggregate.spec.ts`

- **Customer**
  - records its birth with the identity it was checked on, and holds the state that event gives
  - refuses to identify a person whose identity document expired before the day
  - refuses to identify a person whose proof of address is dated more than three months before the day
  - accepts a proof of address dated exactly three months before the day
  - is rebuilt from its history, recording nothing
  - is built by the builder from the identity and the instant it is born
  - records the registration of its business with the facts of the business
  - holds the business it registered, and the facts of it, once the event is applied
  - refuses a second registration of its business, and keeps the first one
- **Customer, once its screening found a possible match, and when its relationship ends**
  - ends the relationship with a customer, recording the instant it ends
  - records no second end of a relationship already ended
  - confirms a possible match as a sanctions match, recording the instant of the confirmation
  - refuses to confirm a customer with no possible match waiting for compliance review
  - dismisses a possible match with the reason of compliance, recording the instant of the dismissal
  - refuses to dismiss a possible match without a reason of compliance, recording nothing
  - refuses to dismiss a customer with no possible match waiting for compliance review
  - refuses to confirm a match twice, the second confirmation records nothing
