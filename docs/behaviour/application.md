## Acceptance specs

### us0 (acceptance), in acceptance

`acceptance/us0.acceptance.spec.ts`

- **US0, acceptance**

## End-to-end specs

### submit-application (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/submit-application.e2e.spec.ts`

- **Submit application, end to end**
  - answers 401 to a caller who presents no credential
  - answers 403 to a caller who is not the customer the application is for
  - lets a customer submit an application: 201, its Location, and its amount in whole cents
  - answers the refusal code of an amount that is not a positive whole number of cents
  - is one trace: the request, the use case and what it saves
  - publishes the integration events other contexts rely on (US0.05) (planned)
