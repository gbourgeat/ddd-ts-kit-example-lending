### application-count (projection), in unit

`unit/application-count.projection.spec.ts`

- **Application count, kept from what credit-application announces**
  - counts one more application and sums its amount on the branch and month of its submission
  - keeps the same line when the same event is delivered twice
  - keeps the newest line when an older event is delivered late
- **Application count, one line per branch and month**
  - adds the applications of a branch and month one after another
  - keeps the applications of another branch on its own line
  - counts a submission on the month of its instant in UTC
  - refuses a submission without its tenant, and counts nothing under no tenant

### decision (projection), in unit

`unit/decision.projection.spec.ts`

- **Decision, counted on its branch and month**
  - counts one more decision, and one more approval, when the decision approves
  - counts a refusal as a decision that is not an approval
  - counts an approval with conditions as an approval
- **Decision, counted for its exception and kept once per event**
  - counts an exception among the decisions of its branch and month
  - keeps the same line when the same event is delivered twice
  - keeps the newest line when an older decision is delivered late
- **Decision without its branch, as a version 1 stored one**
  - skips the decision without refusing it, so that the consumer is never blocked
