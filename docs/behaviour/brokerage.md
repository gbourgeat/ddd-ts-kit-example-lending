### check-registration (handler), in unit

`unit/check-registration.handler.spec.ts`

- **Check registrations**
  - marks as lapsed the broker whose registration is no longer valid on the day of the check
  - leaves unmarked the broker whose registration is still valid on the day of the check
  - answers how many brokers it marked as lapsed
  - announces the lapse of each broker it marks, as its save is made
  - does not mark again a broker already lapsed, so the lapse is announced once
  - saves nothing for a broker whose registration is still valid
  - is refused to an actor who does not hold the permission broker.check

### register-broker (handler), in unit

`unit/register-broker.handler.spec.ts`

- **Register broker**
  - saves the broker with its registration number when the intermediaries register confirms it
